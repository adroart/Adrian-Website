What each one does

Touch a primitive on the phone and its entry lights. Every behavior below is specified as duration, easing, and its three states, so nothing is left to be guessed at build time.

One

The row that opens

The signature move. A hairline row lifts to the top of the page, becomes the header, and its content rises into the space its siblings left. The drawing shrinks and stays, so the piece is never out of sight while you read about it. Nothing navigates. There is no back arrow: the header dropping back into the list is the way out.

Motion
Row travels up over 580ms, siblings fall and fade over 380ms, content rises at 100ms delay over 440ms. All on an exponential ease out.
Reverse
Exact inverse. The same object returning to the same slot is what makes the page feel like one surface.
Loaded
Title as header, content beneath, drawing anchored left.
Empty
Row still opens. One quiet line: nothing written yet.
Error
Row opens to the failure with a retry, never a blocked tap.
Reduced
Crossfade at 120ms, no travel.

Tap any of the four rows.

Two

The brass pill

The only colored thing on any screen, and never more than one per screen. Brass means you can act on this. That single rule is what buys the flow its clarity: a person scanning a screen has exactly one place to look.

Motion
Press scales to 97% over 220ms. Its glow lifts on hover. Never bounces, never pulses to attract.
Loaded
Filled brass, wood-dark label, uppercase Karla at 12px.
Working
Label holds, brass dims to 70%, input blocks.
Disabled
Falls to hairline outline. Loses brass entirely, because brass is a promise you can act.
Arrival
The piece page is one page dressed by relationship, and the pill is how it says which. Unclaimed: a lit Begin, the only bright thing on an otherwise quiet page. Already registered to someone else: no brass at all, the code door drops to a quiet line and Sign in becomes the obvious move. Registered to you: no pill, because there is nothing left to claim.
Begin, not Unlock
Unlock names the mechanism; Begin names what happens, and it honestly covers both openings, because behind it sits registering it to yourself or leaving sealed words for whoever you bought it for.

Press Unlock.

Three

The plain text link

Every escape, every refusal, every departure. Not now. Skip for now. I don’t have a code. Into the artist’s website. The contrast between one solid pill and plain text is the entire hierarchy on a screen, which means a link must never look like a row.

The law
Hairline rows open in place. Text links travel. Anything that leaves this page is a link, never a row.
Sign in
Also a link, under the pill, and only when the piece is already registered. The daily return is the account; the code claims and transfers, then sleeps. Once signed in as this piece’s caretaker the link is gone and Your account is a row.
Motion
Color only, 220ms. No underline animation, no arrow.
Placement
Below the pill, never beside it. Vertical stacking is what makes the hierarchy readable at a glance.

Hover the link under the pill.

Four

The code field

Appears once in the entire journey and is the only input that performs. Six characters resolve one at a time like tumblers finding their places, a pause, then one soft seat on the last. Then the vault opens: the ground parts, warm light comes through, and the light carries you to the next screen. No button press after the click. Being carried through is the reward.

Tumblers
One character every 150ms, each seating with a 5px drop.
Pause
400ms of stillness after the last. The pause is what makes the opening feel earned rather than automatic.
Vault
Ground parts over 1150ms on a slow-in-slow-out curve, light blooms behind it. Whole passage two to four seconds. Never longer.
Wrong
No shake, no red. The field clears and the copy changes to the code is not true, with try again and contact Adrian.
Reduced
Characters appear at once, vault becomes a clean fade.

Press Unlock, then watch. This is the one theatrical moment in the flow, and nothing else competes with its scale. The link beneath it runs a code that will not match, so the refusal of shake and red can be seen rather than trusted.

Five

The promoted surface

What a row becomes. Its own scroll, its own three states, the drawing anchored beside its title. This is also every caretaker door inside the piece page: the garden, the video capsule, the account, piece information. One motion vocabulary, used everywhere, so a person learns the gesture once.

Title
Cormorant 25px, promoted from Lora 15.5px. The type change is how you know it is now a header and not a row.
Scroll
Only the content scrolls. The header and anchor stay fixed. The page itself never scrolls, which is what protects the no-scroll rule.
Depth
One level only. A promoted surface never promotes another.

Open The certificate to see a facts layout, The dreams to see empty.

Six

The glow meter

The only element in the system that changes over years, and the one place the whole page’s aliveness is set from. You feel something, you place it in the piece, and the piece glows brighter: cause and effect, never a request. Nothing counts, nothing nags, nothing asks. The page simply becomes more alive as intention accumulates, and that visible aliveness is the whole invitation. Tapping the light opens the garden.

Form
A line of light that lengthens. No number, no ring, no total, because any of those turn brightness into a score. The line is the light.
Ramp
Length and glow grow together from a visible floor, so an unanswered piece still looks alive. Reaching full is warmth, not completion.
Breath
A 5.5 second glow cycle. Slow enough to feel like light rather than a loading state.
On answer
The line grows over one second, the glow follows at 850ms. Plays once, on return to the page, never on a loop.
Zero
A short lit line, copy reads: the questions are waiting. Never dark, because an unanswered piece is not a failed one.
Promoted
Survives. It thins to a quiet lit line above the header, the way the drawing shrinks, so the caretaker page shows meter and rows at once.
The page
One variable drives everything: the ground lifts from near-black toward warm espresso, the drawing’s stroke thickens and brightens, the name gains a faint glow, and words placed in the piece surface beneath it. A piece with nothing in it is calm and complete, never empty; a piece with years in it reads as lit from within. This is public: a guest scanning a loved piece sees that it has been loved.
Never
A count, a bar, a streak, a badge, or a line asking for anything. The piece is not owed a debt and the caretaker is never behind.

Four rules the primitives depend on

These are the decisions that keep six behaviors from becoming twenty four inconsistent ones. Each was a real ambiguity in the wording record.

Rows open, links travel

The five rows on the piece page were not five of a kind. Four open in place and one leaves the site entirely, while looking identical. Into the artist’s website is now a text link below the list, not a row.

Recommended, open to redirect.

The drawing shrinks, it never leaves

When a row promotes, the piece scales to a small anchor beside the new header. You are never not looking at the piece while reading about the piece, and the header gets weight the type alone could not give it.

Recommended, open to redirect.

One theatrical moment

The vault is the only passage over one and a half seconds. Every other payoff is under it, plays once on entry, and can be interrupted by a tap. The screens ship with plain fades first if that is faster; motion layers in after without changing a single line of copy or layout.

Drawings are stroke paths, not assets

Every CAD drawing draws itself, so each one has to be authored as ordered strokes with a drawing sequence, not exported flat. A flat export kills the motion layer quietly, and it is the easiest thing in this whole document to get wrong.

The twenty four screens

Every screen in walking order, which primitives assemble it, and the one moment that belongs to it alone. Screens with no moment listed are deliberately still.

Two labels in the table are not primitives, and are set in a lighter face to say so. Field is the plain text input on the five gathering screens; it has no motion of its own and inherits whatever the form pattern turns out to be. Tap anywhere is the four introduction screens having no target at all: the whole screen advances. Both are named here so the count of six stays honest.

What is still open

Two decisions are built here on my recommendation and are the fastest things to redirect. Everything else follows the locked wording record.

The photograph

Built as: shrinks to an anchor and stays. The alternatives are that it collapses away entirely, which gives the most room and the cleanest header but loses the piece while you read about it, or that it holds full size with content scrolling under, which is most reverent but breaks the no scroll rule.

The fifth row

Built as: a text link below the list. The alternatives are that it stays a row with a quiet outbound mark, which needs a mark the no icons rule has to allow, or that it opens in place with a short invitation and a real link inside it, which never sends anyone away by accident at the cost of one extra tap.
