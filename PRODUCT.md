# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is Adrian Rasmussen, working as the artist, publisher, seller,
and steward of the artwork registry. He uses the admin to move real work forward:
publishing stories and poetry, preparing private viewings, pricing and invoicing,
and creating permanent artwork identities before pieces leave the studio.

The interface should not require development knowledge or remembering where a
tool lives. It should surface the next meaningful action and preserve context as
Adrian moves between connected tasks.

## Product Purpose

The admin is the private operating system for Adrian Rasmussen Art. It brings the
site's separate tools into one reliable workspace, makes unfinished work visible,
and gives each recurring workflow a clear beginning, middle, and completion.

Success means Adrian can open the admin, immediately understand what needs
attention, begin common tasks in one step, and move between tools without reloads,
session flashes, or navigation dead ends.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, precise.

The admin should feel like a quiet studio desk with the clarity and speed of a
professional operations tool. It remains recognizably connected to the public
Adrian Rasmussen site, but function and legibility lead every design decision.

## Anti-references

- Not a generic SaaS dashboard filled with decorative metrics and interchangeable cards.
- Not a dark technical control room or developer console.
- Not a miniature version of the public portfolio where atmosphere obscures tasks.
- Not a collection of unrelated forms with inconsistent controls and navigation.
- Not a dense enterprise system that requires training to understand.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the next meaningful action.** Home begins with work requiring attention
   and common starting points, not a directory of tools.
2. **Organize around the art practice.** Artwork, Publishing, and Sales are the
   stable mental model. Internal technology names never lead the interface.
3. **Keep the workspace continuous.** Navigation, identity, and context persist
   while the active tool changes.
4. **Make consequential steps explicit.** Issuing plates, activating physical
   records, sending viewings, and recording payments have clear states and feedback.
5. **Use warmth with discipline.** Natural color and restrained typography carry
   the studio character while familiar product patterns keep every task obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, labels, error
messaging, and touch targets. The full admin must work at phone and desktop widths.
Motion is brief and functional, and reduced-motion preferences are respected.
