## Context for after (Adrian's reference only, not for the fresh chat)

Four phases. Phase 0 is the merge that makes one ownership record out of two — it blocks everything else. Phase 1 is the registration a person can walk, including the invitation for collectors who bought years ago. Phase 2 is what makes the piece feel alive. Phase 3 is what only exists on paper.

Each session stops at a phase boundary so you get eyes on it before the next layer goes on top. Phase 0 especially: it moves ownership records, and a provenance system's whole value is that the record cannot be quietly changed.

Two things only you can do, and both gate real work: the materials for each piece, and four calls — what video promises, whether a spouse is a holder or a contributor, what makes a light brighter without being gameable, and what the invitation carries as proof.
