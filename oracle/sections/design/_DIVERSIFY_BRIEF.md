# DESIGN opening-diversification brief

The 64 DESIGN files were generated by parallel agents who pattern-matched on
UL 1's locked samples. Audit caught:

- 24 of 64 centre_field openings start "The centre this gate sits in..." —
  the locked UL 1 sample being copied as a template.
- 8 of 64 channel openings start "Half a circuit..." — same issue.

Each agent working alone produced fine writing; collectively they converged
on the samples. This pass fixes it.

## Your task

For your assigned UL N card, rewrite ONLY the first sentence of the field
you are told to fix. Do not touch other fields. Do not touch other sentences.

If the new opener needs the second sentence to be minimally adjusted for flow,
that is okay — but keep the change small.

## How to rewrite

The new opener must:

1. Do the same work the old opener did (open the field on a relevant move).
2. Avoid the banned formula being fixed AND all other banned patterns
   (no "Gate N is...", no "This gate/code...", no "There is a...", no
   "Something + verb + when...", no "At some point/Eventually/Far enough/One
   day...", no "As a gate, this code is...", no "Half a circuit...", no
   "The centre this gate sits in...").
3. Match the rest of the paragraph in tone and image. Read the whole field
   first.
4. Stay in voice: intimate teacher, plain, no em dashes, no triads.

Varied opening moves (use as seeds, not a template list):

- Plain stative: "Self-love is wired here as behaviour, not as feeling."
- Concrete observation: "Watch how the body settles when this gate fires."
- Centre image: "The throat is the body's town square."
- Centre placement: "Down at the base of the chart, the root..."
- Fragment: "Pure beginning, raw."
- Imperative: "Look at where this gate sits in the body-map."
- Misread named: "Most people read this placement as..."
- Subject-led drive: "Knowing arrives here as..."

## Hard rules

- No em dashes. No en dashes.
- Word count must stay within ceiling (gate/centre_field/channel ≤ 185).
- Valid JSON.

## When done

Report the card number, the field you fixed, the old first sentence, the new
first sentence, the field's word count, and confirm "no em dashes".
