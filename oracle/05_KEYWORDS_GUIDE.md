# 05 — The Keywords Guide

How to write the keyword layer on all 64 cards.

Read `00_MASTER_WRITING_GUIDE.md` first. The master guide holds the two-phase
frame (§1), the terms (§3), the card-coherence doctrine (§5), the copyright
line (§6), the shared voice rules (§7) and the human-writing discipline — all
of which apply here and are not repeated. This document covers only what is
specific to the **keyword layer**.

The keywords are the 5 to 7 brief words printed directly under the art piece.
They are the first words a reader meets. Per master §5.1, their unique job is
to **pinpoint the code at a glance** — and they must never use any single
system's jargon.

---

## 1. The problem this layer fixes

The keywords were being pulled straight from one system — the Gene Keys
frequency words. Two things were wrong with that:

- **They require framework knowledge.** A keyword like "Promethean Fire" only
  means something to a reader who already knows Gene Keys. A keyword that
  needs the system to be understood cannot be the *entrance* to the card. It
  is a locked door, not a portal.
- **They are single-system.** The card is a **code** — one energy across four
  lineages (master §2). A word lifted from Gene Keys reports only the Gene
  Keys view. It cannot pinpoint the energy the I-Ching, the Human Design gate,
  and the Tarot Arcana are *also* holding.

The keyword layer is the one place on the card where the synthesis of the
whole code becomes visible and primary. It must be written in plain language,
owned by Adrian.

(The Gene Keys Shadow / Gift / Siddhi triad is not discarded — it stays, named
within the description prose where it belongs. It is simply not the keyword
layer. See `01`.)

### The current sets are close, not finished

Most cards already carry keyword sets, and most keywords in them already work
— plain, graspable, true. The sets are not broken, they are *unfinished*. Two
recurring faults to correct:
- **Jargon outliers.** A few keywords need framework knowledge to land
  ("The Promethean Spark" on Card 1). These come out.
- **A missed facet of the energy.** A set may track only part of the code —
  often the Gene Keys arc — and miss a face the I-Ching or the gate was also
  holding. The fix is not to "add a system's word"; it is to notice the
  energy that is not yet held, and write a plain word that holds it.

---

## 2. What a keyword is

A keyword here is a **distillation**, not a label. You arrive at it by holding
the whole card — all four mappings and the one-breath truth (master §8, step
1) — and naming the energy in your own, immediately-graspable words.

### The one test: does it hold the energy?

A keyword earns its place by a single test — **does it hold the energy of the
card?** Nothing else. In particular:

- **Provenance does not matter.** It is irrelevant which system a keyword
  "came from," or whether it traces to any system at all. A keyword is not
  tagged to a lineage and does not represent one. The I-Ching, Gene Keys,
  Human Design and Tarot are *consulted* — you read all four so that no facet
  of the energy is missed — but the keyword itself is judged only on whether
  it pinpoints the code.
- **There is no per-system quota.** Do not fish for "an I-Ching word" or "an
  HD word" to fill a slot. That makes the layer mechanical. Look at
  everything, then write words that hold the whole.

So "pull from all the systems" is a *practice* (consult them all, miss no
facet), never a *rule* (one keyword per system). The discipline is the same
as the one-breath truth: take in everything, then name the single energy.

A keyword is the same move as the card name — a "third thing" the mappings
live inside — at the keyword scale. Because it is your own distillation and
not lifted from any source, it is also legally clean.

---

## 3. The form

- **Count:** 5 to 7 keywords per card.
- **Length:** each keyword is brief — a single word, or a two-to-three-word
  phrase acting as one unit. Short.
- **Placement:** directly under the art piece. The threshold of the card.
- **Job:** the at-a-glance pinpoint. A reader glances and the energy lands,
  before any longer text is read.

### Register

- **Mostly plain.** Everyday words anyone grasps instantly. Reachability is
  the point.
- **Occasionally an idea.** One keyword may capture the energy as a small
  idea or image rather than a plain noun — but even then it stays brief.
- **The hard line:** nothing the reader has to stop and decode. A keyword
  should *land*, not puzzle. If a reader has to think about what it means, or
  needs a framework to grasp it, it has failed. No jargon, no system terms,
  no coined vocabulary.

### How the 5 to 7 work together

They are not five synonyms. The set should *triangulate* the code — each
keyword catching the energy from a slightly different angle, so that together
they pinpoint it more precisely than any one could. Some name a feeling, some
a movement, some an image, some a tone. Variety within the set is what makes
it land — and it is also how you catch every facet of the energy without ever
needing a per-system quota.

## 4. Worked example — Card 1, "Earth's Breath" (a real fix)

This is an actual card, corrected — not an invented illustration.

**The current set (9 keywords):**

> Creative Force · Origination · The Void Before Form · Inner Fire ·
> Cyclical Renewal · Gestation · Aliveness · The Promethean Spark ·
> Unity Through Beauty

Card 1's energy: pure originating force, the aliveness that makes the first
move. (I-Ching: the Creative, doubled Heaven, ceaseless motion. Gene Keys:
Entropy to Freshness to Beauty. HD: Gate 1, Self-Expression.)

What is wrong with the current set:
- **"The Promethean Spark"** — needs framework and myth knowledge to land.
  It does not pinpoint plainly. Cut.
- **"The Void Before Form"** — poetic-abstract; a reader pauses on it rather
  than grasping it instantly. Cut.
- **"Cyclical Renewal"** and **"Gestation"** — both lean toward *return* and
  *waiting*. Card 1's charge is the *first* active move, not the cycle back.
  A slight mismatch of energy, and "Cyclical Renewal" overlaps "Origination."
  Cut both.
- That trims 9 to 5. But the set was also missing a facet: the I-Ching's
  *ceaseless first motion* was not held by any keyword.

**The corrected set (7 keywords):**

> Creative Force · Origination · Inner Fire · Aliveness ·
> First Movement · Pure Beginning · Unity Through Beauty

Every keyword lands at a glance, no framework needed. "First Movement" and
"Pure Beginning" were added not because a rule demanded an I-Ching keyword,
but because that facet of the energy — the ceaseless originating motion — was
not yet held. The test was only ever: does it hold the energy of the card?

## 5. Per-card keyword checklist

- [ ] 5 to 7 keywords, each brief (one word or a 2–3 word phrase).
- [ ] Every keyword is plain enough to land instantly — no decoding required.
- [ ] No jargon: nothing from any system's vocabulary that needs the framework
      to be understood, and no coined or myth-dependent terms.
- [ ] Every keyword passes the one test — it holds the energy of the card.
- [ ] All four mappings and the one-breath truth were consulted, so no facet
      of the energy is missed. (Consulted as practice — there is no per-system
      quota; provenance does not matter.)
- [ ] The set triangulates the code from varied angles, not 5–7 synonyms.
- [ ] Master guide §6 (copyright) and §7 (voice) obeyed.
- [ ] Read at a glance. The energy lands without effort.
- [ ] Card status set (master §10): `scaffold` if AI-drafted, `final` once
      Adrian has personally written or reworked it.
