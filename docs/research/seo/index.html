Strategy

The aim is not just to rank for one phrase. The aim is to make Adrian a credible, citable entity for original Mandala art, sacred geometry art, layered wood sculpture, and high-value commissioned works.

Positioning

Lead With Meaning

The highest-value buyers are more likely to respond to sacred geometry, Mandala, original artwork, artist identity, and commissioned space-specific work than to a pure production phrase like laser cut.

Mandala art Sacred geometry Original work

Use Process As Authority
