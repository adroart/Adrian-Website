- [Luminous gemstones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminous_gemstones) documents Chinese compounds such as yèmíngzhū and yèguāngzhū for night luminous or night shining pearls and gems.

## Search Intent Notes

### `mandala art`

Broad and competitive. SERPs are dominated by Pinterest, YouTube, Wikipedia, tutorials, stock image libraries, Etsy-style marketplaces, and large art marketplaces. The opportunity is not to copy them. The opportunity is to become a distinctive artist/entity result for original mandala art.

Best strategy:

- Build one excellent Mandala collection page.
- Add first-hand writing about mandala process and meaning.
- Improve each individual Mandala artwork page.
- Use high-quality images, alt text, structured data, and image sitemap entries.
- Build off-site mentions that associate Adrian with mandala art.

### `sacred geometry art`

Broad but better aligned with Adrian's actual practice. SERPs include artists, workshops, symbol sites, tutorials, and marketplaces. This term can connect Mandala, Light Codes, Universal Language, About, and writings.

Best strategy:

- Use the phrase naturally across Mandala, About, and writings.
- Create a definitive essay from Adrian's lived artistic perspective.
- Use artwork pages to show the term embodied in real objects.

### `laser cut art`

Large but noisy. Many searchers want files, machines, tutorials, or examples. It is useful, but not precise enough by itself.

Best strategy:

- Use the broader term in page titles and copy.
- Make the actual landing page specific: Laser-Cut Wood Art.
- Avoid building file/template content.
- Show finished original artworks and process.

### `laser cut wood art`

Best large material term. It matches the actual inventory and has enough specificity to distinguish Adrian from generic art, tutorial, and file results.

Best strategy:

- New page built at `/creations/laser-cut-wood-art`.
- Link to it from Creations and Multidimensional Art.
- Include all eligible laser-cut wood pieces.
- Use process copy, material facts, structured data, and image sitemap entries.

### `layered wood art`

Good secondary term. It captures the physical depth of the work and overlaps with collector/product intent. It is less spiritually specific than Mandala and less technical than laser-cut, which makes it useful as supporting language.

Best strategy:

- Use on the Laser-Cut Wood Art hub.
- Use on artwork descriptions where the layered construction is visible.
- Include in process writing.

### `geometric wood art`

Potentially high impressions, but mixed intent. A lot of results lean toward DIY or interior object browsing. Use it as supporting language, not the core brand position.

Best strategy:

- Mention naturally where relevant.
- Do not make it the main page title.
- Use "sacred geometry" and "layered laser-cut wood" as stronger differentiators.
