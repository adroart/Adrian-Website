# AI Citation And Brand Presence Playbook

Last updated: 2026-05-20

## Summary

Classic rankings still matter, but they are no longer the whole scorecard. AI systems can answer a searcher's question directly, cite supporting pages, mention brands without sending a click, or shape a later direct visit. The goal is to make Adrian Rasmussen a clear entity associated with:

- Mandala art.
- Original mandala art.
- Sacred geometry art.
- Sacred geometry mandala art.
- Laser-cut wood art.
- Layered wood art.
- Wooden mandala sculpture.
- Multi-dimensional wooden sculpture.
- Bali-based artist.

## What The Transcript Gets Right

The transcript Adrian shared is directionally right in four ways:

1. Organic clicks can drop even when rankings do not.
2. AI answers can satisfy the user before a click happens.
3. Being cited, named, or included in an AI-generated answer is now a meaningful visibility metric.
4. Website SEO alone is not enough. Off-site presence matters because AI systems and search systems evaluate the broader web, not only the brand's own pages.

## What Needs Nuance

Ranking is not obsolete. Google's own AI guide says AI features in Google Search still use the Search index, ranking systems, crawlability, snippets, and content quality. Ranking is the floor. Citation visibility adds another layer on top.

The practical strategy is:

- Build crawlable, high-quality pages on Adrian's own site.
- Make those pages excellent enough to cite.
- Build enough off-site references that the web reinforces Adrian's association with the category.
- Track both rankings and AI answer inclusion.

## Citation Goals

The target is not only "rank number one." The target is to appear in answers to prompts such as:

- Who are contemporary mandala artists making original work?

## Source Types AI Systems Commonly Surface

Based on current AI search behavior and public studies, the strongest source ecosystem includes:

- The artist's own website.
- Google-indexed pages with strong titles, headings, metadata, schema, and content.
- YouTube videos and transcripts.
- Reddit and forum discussions when genuine.
- Pinterest image collections.
- Instagram profile and post context.
- Artist directories.
- Gallery or exhibition pages.
- Podcast pages and transcripts.
- Interviews.
- Articles by independent publications.
- Marketplace pages only when the product data is accurate.
- Business profiles and local/entity pages.

## Content Qualities AI Can Cite

Every major page should have:

- A direct definition of what the work is.
- A clear material/process explanation.
- Specific first-hand details.
- Short standalone sentences that can be cited accurately.
- Structured sections with headings.
- Images near relevant text.
- Internal links to related pages.
- Schema that matches visible content.
